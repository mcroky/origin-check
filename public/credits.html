Barcode scanning & live lookup

Barcode camera frames and photos stay on-device. Lookup sends only the barcode, interface language and app identifier to Open Food Facts using API v3.6. Source records are community-contributed and may be incomplete or outdated. The app does not upload photos or edit the public database. Barcode lookup results retain original fields, timestamps and source links. ODbL 1.0 / DbCL 1.0 apply to returned records.
